# Titles that still repeat after deduplication (review, no data changed)

Reviewed on the 1,975-paper corpus in `ingest/data/papers_pilot.jsonl`. **37 titles occur twice** when titles are compared on letters and digits only (`[^a-z0-9]` removed). `ingest/deduplicate.py` removes punctuation but keeps spaces and hyphens apart ("low-resource" and "low resource" differ), so by its own comparison there are 34; the other 3 differ only in such spelling. No title occurs three times.

**My reading of each pair** (from the authors, years, venues and DOIs below; a person has not checked the papers themselves): **26 are the same paper** (a preprint and its published version, a repository or video copy, or a reprint), **7 are different papers** that share a title, and **4 are borderline** (an extended journal version of a workshop or conference paper, or a book and its review).

## Why the deduplication left them

`deduplicate.py` merges two records with the same title only if they share an author word, are at most 3 years apart **and, when both have an abstract, the abstracts overlap by at least 0.25 (Jaccard on words of 4+ letters)**. In most pairs below the abstracts overlap by 0.00 to 0.05 although the authors, year and title are identical: the arXiv record carries the full abstract and the published record carries a short, different text (200 to 260 characters). The abstract test is what keeps these duplicates apart. Tried on these 37 pairs only (not on the whole corpus): a rule of "same title, at least 3 shared author words and a year gap of 1 or less" would merge 21 of the 26 duplicates, 0 of the 7 different papers and 3 of the 4 borderline pairs. The duplicates it misses have a year gap above 1 (a data-repository copy years later, a reprint) or fewer than 3 shared author words (one record lists only the first author or the conference as author). The borderline pairs need a person. Not changed here.

Effect: these duplicate pairs sit next to each other in search results (for example `W4411120615` and `W4404573715`, or `W4412887109` and `W4414581550`). `collapse_same_title` hides them at query time; it does not repair the data.
